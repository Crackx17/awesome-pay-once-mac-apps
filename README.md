## Why Pay Once?

Subscriptions made sense for cloud services with ongoing server costs. But a text editor? A Git client? A disk space visualizer? These apps run locally on your Mac — there's no reason they need to charge you every month.

Pay-once apps:
- **Respect your purchase** — you own what you paid for
- **No surprise charges** — your credit card stays in your wallet
- **Indie-friendly** — many are built by solo developers or small teams
- **Better incentives** — developers earn money by shipping great updates, not by locking features behind tiers
